# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

## Overview

SpendLens is a privacy-first personal finance application that helps you track expenses by analyzing your transaction SMS messages. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection

### SMS Messages
- **What we collect**: Transaction SMS messages from banks, payment apps, and credit cards
- **How we collect**: With your explicit permission via Android's READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS permissions
- **What we extract**: Transaction amount, date, merchant name, and category
- **What we DON'T collect**: Account numbers, full SMS content, or personal identifiers

### No Account Required
SpendLens does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as:
- Email address
- Phone number  
- Name or address
- Financial account numbers

## Data Storage & Security

### On-Device Storage Only
- All data is stored **exclusively on your device**
- No data is transmitted to any server or cloud service
- No internet connection is used for core functionality

### Encryption
- Database is encrypted using SQLCipher with 256-bit AES encryption
- Encryption keys are protected by Android Keystore System
- Data is encrypted at rest and in memory when possible

### No Cloud Backup
- Cloud backup is explicitly disabled
- Data transfer between devices is not supported
- Your data never leaves your device

## Internet Usage

The standard version of SpendLens does not require or use internet permissions. However, some features may use network access:

### Optional Features (if enabled)
- **Merchant name resolution**: If enabled, the app may send only merchant name tokens to a lookup service to get standardized merchant names
- **Crash reporting**: If enabled, anonymous crash reports may be sent to help improve the app

### No Personal Data Transmitted
When internet is used, we never send:
- Transaction amounts
- Account information
- Full SMS content
- Any personally identifiable information

## Your Rights

### Data Control
- **Access**: You can view all your transactions within the app
- **Delete**: You can delete individual transactions or all data at once through the app settings
- **Export**: You can export your transaction data (if this feature is available)
- **Opt-out**: You can revoke SMS permissions at any time through Android settings

### Data Deletion
To delete all your data:
1. Open SpendLens
2. Go to Settings
3. Select "Delete All Data"
4. Confirm the deletion

This permanently removes all transactions and app data from your device.

## Third-Party Services

SpendLens may use the following third-party services:

### Google Play Services
- Required for app functionality on Android devices
- Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Crash Reporting (if enabled)
- New Relic or similar services may be used for crash reporting
- Only anonymous crash data and device information are collected
- Can be disabled in app settings

## Children's Privacy

SpendLens is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
