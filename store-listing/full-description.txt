📱 **SpendLens — Automatic Expense Tracker via SMS**

Stop manually entering every transaction. SpendLens reads your bank and payment SMS messages automatically and categorizes your spending — so you can focus on managing your money, not logging it.

✨ **KEY FEATURES**

🔹 **Automatic Transaction Capture**
- Reads transaction SMS from banks, UPI apps, wallets, and credit cards
- Extracts amount, merchant, category, and account details instantly
- No manual entry required — works in the background

🔹 **Smart Categorization**
- AI-powered auto-categorization learns your spending habits
- Pre-built patterns for 100+ Indian banks and payment providers
- Customize categories and rules to match your preferences

🔹 **Privacy-First Design**
- All data stored locally on your device (SQLCipher encrypted)
- No account required — your data stays on your phone
- Works offline — no internet needed for core features

🔹 **Comprehensive Spending Insights**
- Track spending by category, merchant, and time period
- Visual charts and graphs to understand your financial habits
- Set budget goals and monitor progress

🔹 **Secure & Protected**
- App lock with biometric authentication (fingerprint/face unlock)
- Encrypted database prevents unauthorized access
- No cloud storage unless you explicitly enable backup

🔹 **Backup & Restore**
- Export encrypted backups to your device
- Restore anytime when switching phones
- Your data remains under your control

🎯 **WHO IS THIS FOR?**

- Want to track spending without manual entry
- Use multiple bank accounts and cards
- Need quick insights into where your money goes
- Value privacy and don't want cloud-based finance apps
- Want a simple, ad-free expense tracker

🏦 **SUPPORTED PROVIDERS**

Works with major Indian banks and payment services:
- Banks: HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, and 100+ more
- UPI: GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BharatPe, Cred, and others
- Credit Cards: All major card providers
- Wallets: Paytm Wallet, Amazon Pay, Mobikwik

🔒 **PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED**

- **READ_SMS**: Required to read transaction messages from banks/payment apps
- **RECEIVE_SMS**: Needed to capture new transactions automatically
- **INTERNET**: Used only for optional features (AI categorization, pattern sync, analytics)

📊 **HOW IT WORKS**

1. Install SpendLens and grant SMS permission
2. The app automatically detects and reads transaction SMS
3. Transactions are parsed and categorized instantly
4. View your spending analytics in beautiful charts
5. Set budgets and track your financial goals

💡 **PRO TIPS**

- Keep SMS permissions enabled for automatic capture
- Enable biometric lock for extra security
- Review categorized transactions and adjust as needed
- Use pattern sync to get the latest bank SMS formats

🌟 **NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED**

Unlike other finance apps, SpendLens works without creating an account. Your data lives on your device and stays private. Optionally backup to your personal storage.
