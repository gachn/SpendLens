## FAQ for Store Listing

### Q: Does SpendLens upload my SMS to the cloud?
A: No. All data is stored locally on your device with encryption. Only pattern data (not your transactions) is optionally synced to improve SMS parsing.

### Q: Is my financial data secure?
A: Yes. SpendLens uses SQLCipher encryption for local storage and supports biometric app lock. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export a backup.

### Q: Does SpendLens work offline?
A: Yes. Core features work completely offline. Internet is only needed for optional AI categorization and pattern sync.

### Q: Can I export my data?
A: Yes. You can export encrypted backups to your device and restore them anytime.

### Q: Which banks does SpendLens support?
A: SpendLens works with 100+ banks and payment providers in India. It learns new formats automatically via pattern sync.
